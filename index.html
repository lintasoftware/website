Your Technology Partner from South India

Lintasoft is an IT solutions company with a corporate office in Newark, Delaware (USA), a registered office in Kakkanad, Kochi — home to Infopark and one of South India's fastest-growing technology ecosystems — and a branch office at Hanudev Infotech Park in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. We work with startups, SMEs, and enterprises across the globe to turn ideas into reliable software products.

From custom web and mobile applications to cloud infrastructure and IT consulting, our team combines modern engineering practices with a practical, business-first approach. We focus on delivering solutions that are scalable, maintainable, and genuinely useful.
